About This Project (AI Workshop Demo)

The Problem

College students frequently lend small sums of money to friends (canteen, notes, trips). Over time, they forget who owes them, the exact balances, or due dates, causing unnecessary awkwardness.

The Solution

Friend Money Tracker provides a clean digital dashboard to record loans, track partial repayments automatically, send non-aggressive reminders, and keep full borrower profile histories.

The AI Component

Integrated rule-based AI financial intelligence that calculates collection ratios, analyzes risks, generates customized polite reminder templates, and provides interactive Q&A. Structured modularly for instant OpenAI / Gemini API integration!
